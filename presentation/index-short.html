My approach

Two neighborhoods, two playbooks

The Dunes

  • Bilingual by default — Spanish-first, not translated
  • Lead with health — clinics, food, kids
  • Trusted messengers — schools, faith, promotoras
  • Print, SMS, in-person — meet people where they are

Palm Haven

  • Email + digital — higher data and device access
  • Lead with civic engagement — volunteering, advocacy
  • Self-serve resources — links, portals, on-demand
  • Monthly newsletter — opt-in, longer form

By day 90: no resident gets the wrong message in the wrong language on the wrong channel.

[01:40–02:50] So what does this mean for me as your Communications Director? Two playbooks, not one. The Dunes — bilingual by default. Spanish-first content, not translated as an afterthought. Health is the through-line. Trusted messengers — schools, faith leaders, promotoras de salud — do the heavy lifting. Print, SMS, and in-person, not email-first. Palm Haven — email and digital are fine. Lead with civic engagement, advocacy, volunteering. Self-serve resources work. Monthly newsletter, longer form, opt-in. The first ninety days I audit, build the bilingual workflow, and re-mix the channels. Goal: by day ninety, no resident gets the wrong message in the wrong language on the wrong channel.
