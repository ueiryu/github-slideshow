Equity in practice

Two neighborhoods, two playbooks

The Dunes

  • Spanish-first content — not translated, written; plain language, 6th-grade reading level
  • Lead with health — clinics, food access, family programs
  • Trusted messengers — schools, faith leaders, promotoras
  • Print + SMS + in-person — meet people on the channels they actually use

Palm Haven

  • WCAG-AA digital — screen-reader friendly, captioned, keyboard-accessible
  • Lead with civic engagement — volunteering, advocacy, town halls
  • Self-serve resources — portals, on-demand video, longer-form briefs
  • Monthly newsletter — opt-in, data-rich, transparent

By day 90 — every Clear City resident reached on the right channel, in the right language, at the right reading level.
