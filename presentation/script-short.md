# Speaker Script — 3-Minute Cut

**Total runtime: 3:00** · 4 slides · ~360 words at a measured pace

This is the lean cut. Every sentence has to earn its keep. If you find yourself wanting to add a thought, cut a different one — don't try to fit more in.

Open `presentation/index-short.html`. Press `s` for presenter mode, `f` for fullscreen.

---

## Slide 1 — Title (0:00 – 0:25) · 25 seconds

> Hi, I'm [your name]. [pause] In three minutes I'll show you why these two neighborhoods need two completely different communications strategies — and what I'd do about it.

**Coaching note:** Don't waste a single word on small talk. Lead with the thesis.

---

## Slide 2 — Five gaps, one picture (0:25 – 1:40) · 75 seconds

> Five metrics, both neighborhoods, one image. The steeper the line, the bigger the gap. [pause]
>
> The headline: **fifty-nine percent** of Dunes residents have elevated BMI versus **twenty-three percent** in Palm Haven. A **thirty-six-point gap**. [long pause]
>
> And it doesn't stop there. Sixty-nine percent of Dunes residents earn under twenty-five thousand a year. Forty-five percent of Palm Haven earns over forty thousand. **Income at opposite ends.** [pause]
>
> The Dunes also skews younger, and is two-and-a-half times more likely to use Spanish as the primary language at home. [pause]
>
> Same county, same services on offer. **Profoundly different starting points.**

**Coaching note:** This is your data slide. Numbers slow, conclusions fast. The "long pause" after the 36-point gap is your most important silence — give it three full seconds.

---

## Slide 3 — Two playbooks (1:40 – 2:50) · 70 seconds

> So what does this mean for me as your Communications Director? **Two playbooks, not one.** [pause]
>
> The Dunes — **bilingual by default**. Spanish-first, not translated as an afterthought. Health is the through-line. Trusted messengers — schools, faith leaders, promotoras de salud — do the heavy lifting. Print, SMS, and in-person. Not email-first. [pause]
>
> Palm Haven — email and digital are fine. **Lead with civic engagement.** Self-serve resources work. Monthly newsletter, longer form, opt-in. [pause]
>
> The first ninety days I audit, build the bilingual workflow, and re-mix the channels. **The goal is simple — by day ninety, no resident gets the wrong message in the wrong language on the wrong channel.**

**Coaching note:** This is the slide that makes them want to hire you. Every other slide is setup. Land the closing line clean — memorize it.

---

## Slide 4 — Close (2:50 – 3:00) · 10 seconds

> Thank you. [pause] Happy to take questions.

**Coaching note:** Don't apologize. Don't recap. Don't add a "so in conclusion." Just thank them and stop.

---

## Time budget at a glance

| Slide | Content | Time | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title + thesis | 0:25 | 0:25 |
| 2 | Slope chart + data points | 1:15 | 1:40 |
| 3 | Two playbooks + 90 days | 1:10 | 2:50 |
| 4 | Close | 0:10 | 3:00 |

If you're running long in rehearsal, the first thing to cut is one of the data points on slide 2 (drop "two-and-a-half times more likely to use Spanish" — keep BMI and income). That buys you 8 seconds.

## If you have a few extra seconds

In a real presentation, audiences applaud or react. You may finish at 2:45 instead of 3:00. That's fine — silence is better than padding. The discipline of the lean cut is *not filling the time*.
