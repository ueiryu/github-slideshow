My approach

Two neighborhoods, two playbooks

The Dunes

  • Bilingual by default — Spanish-first, not Spanish-translated
  • Lead with health — clinics, food access, kids' programs
  • Trusted messengers — schools, faith leaders, promotoras
  • Print + SMS + in-person — meet people where they are

Palm Haven

  • Email + digital — higher data and device access
  • Lead with civic engagement — volunteering, advocacy
  • Self-serve resources — links, portals, on-demand
  • Newsletter cadence — monthly, opt-in, longer form
So what does this mean for me as your Communications Director? Two playbooks, not one. For The Dunes — bilingual by default. Spanish-first content, not translated-as-an-afterthought. Health is the through-line. Trusted messengers — schools, faith leaders, promotoras de salud — do the heavy lifting. Print, SMS, and in-person. Not email-first. For Palm Haven — email and digital are fine. Lead with civic engagement, advocacy, volunteering. Self-serve resources work — they have the bandwidth and the time. Monthly newsletter, longer form, opt-in.
First 90 days

What I'd do in the first three months

  1. Days 1–30 — Audit. Inventory every channel, every piece of content, every list. Translate nothing until I understand what we have.
  2. Days 31–60 — Build the bilingual workflow. Hire or contract a Spanish-language editor. Set a Spanish-first content calendar for The Dunes.
  3. Days 61–90 — Re-mix the channels. Stand up SMS for The Dunes. Migrate Palm Haven to a single, well-designed email program. Retire the channels that aren't earning their keep.

The goal: by day 90, no resident gets the wrong message in the wrong language on the wrong channel.

And here's how I'd sequence it. First thirty days — audit. I don't translate anything, change anything, or launch anything until I understand what's already in market. Next thirty — build the bilingual workflow. Hire or contract a Spanish-language editor. Build the calendar. Final thirty — re-mix the channels. SMS for The Dunes. A clean email program for Palm Haven. Sunset what isn't working. By day ninety, the goal is simple — no resident gets the wrong message in the wrong language on the wrong channel.
